# Global Market Opportunity & Business Decision Platform

**Should you enter this market, test the opportunity, or invest elsewhere?**

The **Global Market Opportunity & Business Decision Platform** transforms e-commerce data into actionable business intelligence, helping businesses evaluate product opportunities, understand market demand, assess profitability, and make data-driven market entry decisions.

By combining data analysis, financial indicators, and machine learning, the platform turns complex market data into clear, evidence-based recommendations.

### Live Platform

[**Launch the Interactive Dashboard**](https://business--analysis.streamlit.app/)

[![Streamlit App](https://static.streamlit.io/badges/streamlit_badge_black_white.svg)](https://business--analysis.streamlit.app/)

### Key Capabilities

* **Market Opportunity Analysis:** Evaluate product demand, market performance, category trends, and growth opportunities.
* **Machine Learning Predictions:** Estimate product success probability using Random Forest classification models.
* **Profitability & Pricing Analysis:** Assess pricing strategies, unit economics, and financial feasibility.
* **Market Gap Identification:** Discover underserved categories and potential opportunities across product markets.
* **Interactive Decision Intelligence:** Adjust business assumptions and evaluate how changing conditions affect recommendations.
* **Bilingual Interface:** Explore the platform in English or Arabic.

### Business Recommendations

The platform translates its analysis into four actionable outcomes:

* **ENTER** — Market indicators support potential entry, subject to validation.
* **TEST** — Validate demand through a limited pilot before scaling.
* **WATCH** — Monitor market conditions and gather additional evidence.
* **AVOID** — Current indicators suggest an unattractive or high-risk opportunity.

### Data & Technology

The platform integrates multiple e-commerce datasets covering Brazilian marketplace transactions, global retail performance, and UK online retail activity.

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Random Forest, Streamlit, and data visualization.

### Project Objective

Bridge the gap between raw e-commerce data and practical business strategy through an interactive decision-support platform that helps users evaluate opportunities before committing resources.

**The goal: turn market data into better business decisions.**
